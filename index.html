Arrow keys or WASD to steer, or swipe on the board. Space, P or the Pause button pauses. The cat changes colour with every cocktail. Walls, your own tail and aliens end the game, and the aliens hunt the cat. A faint alien inside a dotted ring is still beaming in and can't hurt you yet.
